# Brag Plan: CroFu

## What is this app?
CroFu provides daily wholesale vegetable price forecasts for India (Potato, Onion, Tomato, Brinjal) 1–30 days ahead using walk-forward validated machine learning models.

## The angle
Quiet, premium data journalism. It takes something normally dry (wholesale agriculture prices) and treats it with the elegance and precision of a Bloomberg terminal or a high-end financial publication.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A massive, elegant gold serif number ("₹2,475.50") typing onto the screen, resolving into a bold forecast claim about tomato prices.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The commodity grid (Tomato, Onion, Potato, Brinjal) appearing one by one with clean, minimalist photography.
- Simulating a click on "National" vs "Tamil Nadu" and watching the forecast numbers and model accuracy stats (MAPE) instantly update.
- The 4-step Method pipeline (Ingest, Engineer, Route, Bound) flashing through in quick succession.

## Outro / punchline
"Four crops. Two markets. Zero guesswork." settling on the screen before fading to the CroFu logo.

## User flow worth showing
The user selects a region (National) and a crop (Tomato) from the minimalist grid → The giant forecast price updates instantly → The model confidence (XGBoost, MAPE 6.4%) is revealed.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: Quiet premium data journalism film
- Interpretation: Restrained pacing. Elegance through typography. Smooth, slow zooms and clean crossfades. Nothing rushed.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0f1613 (Dark mode)
- Accent: #e0ac4c (Gold), #3e8b63 (Brand Green)
- Text: #ece7d9 (Ink)
- Display font: Fraunces
- Body font: IBM Plex Sans
- Strongest visual element: The massive gold Fraunces typography for prices and the grayscale crop cards.

## Share copy (draft)
Stop guessing wholesale vegetable prices. CroFu predicts daily rates for India's major crops up to 30 days ahead with walk-forward validated machine learning.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents
- Music: warm corporate bed
- Music treatment: Low volume, fading in smoothly, maintaining a steady, confident rhythm without overpowering.
- Music cue guidance: 3 strong cues at 0:02, 0:08, and 0:14 for major scene transitions.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS to gently breathe the opacity of the background grain.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched; professional restraint (soft clicks for UI interactions).
- Audio-coupled moments: The typing of the initial forecast number, soft UI clicks during the commodity selection.
- Restraint rule: No loud swooshes or chaotic alarms. Keep it elegant.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Hook — 4s
The screen starts dark. A massive gold serif number `₹2,475.50` types in rapidly in the center of the screen, followed by `per Quintal · expected Aug 2`. The text `Tomatoes are expected to rise 6.7%` slides in below.
Sequential/interaction: The number types out character by character.
Audio intent: Confident, precise.
Audio-coupled idea: Subtle keyboard/ticker ticks as the price types out.
Music: Warm bed fades in.
Transition mood: smooth crossfade → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Crops — 5s
The 4 commodity cards (Tomato, Onion, Potato, Brinjal) slide in from the bottom one by one. The photography is desaturated, looking highly editorial.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 cards arrive one by one.
Audio intent: Clean, rhythmic.
Audio-coupled idea: card-by-card sequence snap sounds on beat.
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The Interaction — 6s
Close up on the coverage section. A simulated cursor clicks "TAMIL NADU". The price on screen flips immediately from ₹2,475 to ₹2,415, and the model stats change to show "ARIMA · MAPE 5.2%".
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulate tap on region button, then price flips.
Audio intent: Tactical, responsive.
Audio-coupled idea: Tap sound for the click, soft digital tick for the price update.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 5s
The screen clears to dark green (`#0f1613`). The words "Four crops. Two markets. Zero guesswork." fade in, word by word. They hold for 2 seconds, then dissolve into the CroFu logo with a gold dot.
Sequential/interaction: yes — sentences reveal progressively.
Audio intent: Finality, polish.
Audio-coupled idea: none.
Music: swell and fade out.
Transition mood: fade to black.
