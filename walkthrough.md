## App Logo Specification

The App Logo is **pure raw typography** (**`CroFu.`**), matching the exact design in the web application:
- **Typography**: Fraunces bold serif font (`font-serif`)
- **Text Color**: Adaptive ink color (`#1c231e` in light mode, `#ece7d9` in dark mode)
- **Signature Dot**: Amber gold accent dot (`#b8872e` in light mode, `#e0ac4c` in dark mode)
